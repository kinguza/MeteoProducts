Where the data comes from

Data sources

Two authoritative global reanalysis products, fetched directly from their publishers.

NOAA

NCEP/NCAR Reanalysis I

Daily global pressure-level and surface reanalysis from NOAA's Physical Sciences Laboratory. Eight decades of data, from 1948 to the present, on a 2.5° grid.

Variables: shum, uwnd, vwnd, air, omega, hgt, rhum (pressure) · slp, pres.sfc, pr_wtr.eatm, *.sig995 (surface)
Source: downloads.psl.noaa.gov

CHIRPS

CHIRPS v2.0

Climate Hazards Group InfraRed Precipitation with Station data. Daily global rainfall on a 0.25° grid, from 1981 to near-present, blending satellite imagery with ground stations.

Variable: precip (mm/day, −50°S to 50°N)
Source: data.chc.ucsb.edu

What the platform handles

Capabilities

Everything a researcher or extension officer needs to work with the data, in one place.

✓
17 variables across 2 data sources Pressure-level reanalysis, surface variables, satellite rainfall.
✓
17 standard pressure levels 1000 mb down to 10 mb, per-variable (humidity stops at 300 mb, omega at 100 mb).
✓
8 temporal aggregations Daily, monthly, seasonal, annual, dekad, pentad, climatology, anomaly.
✓
3 plot types per combination Spatial maps, time series, and Hovmoller (longitude–time) diagrams.
✓
Any point or region on Earth Click a single grid cell for a time series, or draw any bounding box for a regional map.
✓
Daily incremental updates Cron + admin button pull only new days, merging them into the existing catalog in seconds.
✓
One-time full backfill Process the full historical record (1948–present for NOAA, 1981–present for CHIRPS) with a single command.
✓
Resumable & idempotent Re-running the pipeline skips files it already produced, so interrupted runs pick up where they left off.
Agromet · Reanalysis

A unified pipeline for NOAA NCEP reanalysis and CHIRPS rainfall over East Africa. Spatial maps, time series, and Hovmoller diagrams for agrometeorological research and extension work. Built with CDO, xarray, matplotlib, FastAPI, and Supabase.
